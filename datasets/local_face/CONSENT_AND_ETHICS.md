# Consent and ethics statement

Written consent was collected on paper from the recorded participants. The
signed forms contain personal information and are retained privately by the
dataset owner.

The public repository contains only dataset statistics, split metadata and
access instructions. Identifiable images are stored in an encrypted archive.
The password is supplied only after a research request and acceptance of the
data-use terms.

Data access and reuse must remain within the purposes authorized by the
participant consent and any applicable institutional requirements.
