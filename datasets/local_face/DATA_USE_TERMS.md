# Controlled research data-use terms

Access is granted only for the approved face-detection research described in
the access request.

The recipient agrees to:

1. Store the archive and extracted data securely.
2. Restrict access to approved members of the stated research project.
3. Use the data only for face detection and directly related computer-vision
   research.
4. Avoid face recognition, identity inference, biometric profiling and
   participant re-identification.
5. Avoid surveillance, law-enforcement and unauthorized commercial uses.
6. Never redistribute the images, annotations, archive or password.
7. Avoid publishing identifiable source images unless separately authorized.
8. Delete local copies when the approved project ends or access is withdrawn.
9. Report any loss or unauthorized disclosure to the dataset owner.
10. Cite the associated paper in publications using the dataset.

The dataset is supplied without warranty. Access may be withdrawn when these
terms are violated.
